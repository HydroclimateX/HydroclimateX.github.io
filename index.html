Modelling & Forecasting

We advance the prediction of extreme hydrometeorological events — floods, droughts, and compound hazards — by developing wavelet-based spectral transformation methods, multi-model ensemble frameworks, and physics-aware machine learning. Research Interests

Five pillars of hydroclimate extremes research

Extreme Event Simulation & Prediction

Simulation and prediction of extreme hydro-meteorological events using wavelet-based spectral transformation, machine learning, and climate-informed statistical methods. From heavy precipitation to compound flood–drought cascades at seasonal to decadal horizons.

Multi-Agent & Human–Water Systems

Multi-agent models and human–water system simulation capturing feedbacks between reservoir operations, agricultural water use, flood management policies, and climate variability. Agent-based frameworks for coupled natural–human system dynamics.

Landslide Hazard Prediction & Risk

Rainfall-induced landslide hazard prediction and risk assessment integrating satellite precipitation estimates, soil moisture dynamics, and terrain susceptibility mapping. Probabilistic early-warning systems for mountainous regions.

Hydrological–Hydrodynamic–Ecological Modelling

Hydrological–hydrodynamic, hydro-ecological, and water quality process modelling across scales. Coupling land-surface processes with channel routing, reservoir regulation, and nutrient transport for integrated watershed management.

Climate Model Post-Processing & Bias Correction

Post-processing and bias correction of climate and weather forecast models using frequency-domain quantile mapping (WQM). Correcting systematic bias in both trend and variability while preserving the predictive signal across GCM and NWP outputs.
