WQM · Wavelet-based Quantile Mapping

Correct forecast bias across scales.

WQM post-processes precipitation forecasts in the time-frequency domain, correcting amplitude distributions at individual scales before reconstructing an ensemble of rainfall forecasts.

An authentic, bounded demonstration

The online application visualises results generated by WQM 0.1.4 from its official two-station sample. It is designed for method inspection; use the R package for new datasets and research workflows.

Methodology

Decompose → Correct → Rephase → Reconstruct.

01

Decompose

Apply a continuous wavelet transform to observed and forecast precipitation.

02

Correct

Use quantile mapping to correct the wavelet amplitude distribution at each scale.

03

Rephase

Preserve corrected structure while phase shuffling represents timing uncertainty.

04

Reconstruct

Invert the adjusted time-frequency fields into an ensemble of precipitation forecasts.
